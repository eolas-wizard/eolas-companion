# Game Discovery Template

Use this before beginning a new Companion Pack.

## Game identity

- Title
- Genre
- Platforms
- Multiplayer or single-player considerations
- Update cadence

## Player questions

- What do players repeatedly leave the game to research?
- What decisions block progress?
- What information is useful in the moment?
- What should remain discoverable rather than optimized away?

## Progress systems

- Regions
- Quests or objectives
- Collectibles
- Creatures or characters
- Bosses
- Skills or research trees
- Crafting and resources
- Relationships or factions

## Optimization systems

- Builds
- Passive traits
- Equipment
- Production chains
- Breeding or inheritance
- Upgrade and condensation equivalents

## Companion modules

Identify which platform modules apply and which game-specific modules are required.
