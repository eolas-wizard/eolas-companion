# Team Charter

Eolas work is organized into specialist workstreams coordinated through one integrated release plan.

## Product & UX — The Compass

**Mission:** Help the player answer a question or take an action quickly.

Owns navigation, information architecture, mobile flows, accessibility, onboarding, and interaction standards.

## Knowledge

**Mission:** Provide useful, trustworthy game information without recreating an overwhelming wiki.

Owns Companion Pack profiles, mechanics, acquisition methods, items, regions, and practical guidance.

## Progression

**Mission:** Track what matters to the player, not merely what exists in the game.

Owns status dependencies, counts, collectibles, bosses, milestones, condensation, and completion calculations.

## Intelligence

**Mission:** Turn knowledge and progress into useful next-step guidance.

Owns recommendations, Today’s Adventure, optimization, passive guidance, and next-best objectives.

## World

**Mission:** Make exploration easier without removing discovery.

Owns regions, markers, routes, resources, spawn information, and future maps.

## Visual Design

**Mission:** Make Eolas feel consistent, inviting, and premium.

Owns typography, spacing, themes, icons, animation, visual tokens, and component appearance.

## Engineering

**Mission:** Keep the platform reliable, maintainable, responsive, and installable.

Owns architecture, performance, local storage, offline caching, exports, manifests, and GitHub Pages deployment.

## Quality Assurance

**Mission:** Prevent regressions and validate the actual mobile play experience.

Owns responsive, browser, accessibility, navigation, persistence, and release testing.

## Gameplay Research

**Mission:** Discover the questions players genuinely ask while playing.

Owns source review, mechanic validation, patch impact, playtest themes, and research gaps.

## Leadership

- **Creative Director & Lead Playtester:** sets product direction and records contextual feedback in the Alpha Notebook.
- **Technical Product Lead:** coordinates workstreams, integrates releases, digests notebook exports, and maintains roadmap decisions.
