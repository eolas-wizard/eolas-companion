# Product Vision

Eolas is the modern digital evolution of the premium completionist strategy guide.

## North Star

> A strategy guide should never get in the way of the adventure. It should make the adventure richer.

## Every page answers one question

- Continue Adventure: **What should I do next?**
- Regional Chapter: **What should I know here?**
- Pal profile: **How do I obtain and use this Pal?**
- Field Journal: **What is unique to my adventure?**
- Settings: **How should the guide feel while I use it?**
